<gl>The animation has three phases</gl>

<h1>Phase I — Birth</h1>

The shaft appears.

<>h1>Phase II — Formation</h1>

Hundreds of fibers grow outward.

<h1>Phase III — Awakening</h1>

The central eye activates and the repulsion field briefly pushes the surrounding fibers outward.

Then the energy disappears and the feather settles.

That last part is particularly important for a professional animation: the effect shouldn't remain screaming on screen forever.


The key idea is:

each feather filament starts from the shaft → approaches the eye → senses the field → bends around it → continues outward.

Mathematically, we use a Gaussian falloff:

F(d)=Ae^−d2/(2σ2)

#make the field stronger✨
sigma = 1.05
strength = 0.42
